title: Marginalia
oneLiner: Ask a hosted reader a question about a PDF corpus and get claims whose quotes are verbatim, highlighted on the page, with an inspectable retrieval trace and a calibrated refusal.
stack:
  - Python 3.12
  - FastAPI
  - PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector
  - React + Vite
  - PDF.js
  - ONNX Runtime
claim: >-
  The keyed production eval measured retrieval recall@8 at 18/24 (75.0%), the
  answer rate at 15/24 (62.5%), refusal of unanswerable questions at 6/6, and
  citation precision and faithfulness at 13/15 and 12/15.
what: >-
  A Python 3.12 FastAPI service with PostgreSQL 16 and pgvector, served with a
  React reader. Ingest refuses a PDF whose SHA-256 differs from
  corpus.lock.json; retrieval fuses embeddings and lexical search, then
  reranks; the answer path keeps a claim when its quote appears in the cited
  chunk and drops it otherwise. The /ready report counts five papers and 260
  chunks.
hardPart: >-
  A retrieval answer is easy to make plausible and hard to check. A claim can
  cite a passage outside the retrieved set, quote text outside the cited chunk,
  or survive a question the corpus holds no evidence for. The refusal path is
  what makes an answer checkable.
decision:
  title: An uncited claim is dropped, not repaired
  choice: >-
    Validate each claim against its citations, require a verbatim quote from
    the cited chunk, and drop the claim on either failure.
  rejected: >-
    Generate first, then attach the nearest passage; a citation added
    afterwards can point at text outside the claim.
  cost: >-
    The validator drops a reply whose quote is not a verbatim substring, so the
    answer rate is 15/24.
  evidence: >-
    The citation tests cover the rejection path; the eval records answer rate,
    citation precision and faithfulness for every run.
failures:
  - summary: >-
      A fluent answer cites a passage outside the retrieved set, or quotes text
      outside the chunk it names.
    mechanism: >-
      Every claim is validated against its citations: the cited passage must be
      in the retrieved context, and the quote must be a verbatim substring of
      that chunk. A claim that misses either check is dropped before the answer
      is returned; the refusal threshold is read from eval/calibration.json at
      boot.
measured: >-
  The keyed eval measured the five gates: recall@8 18/24, answer rate 15/24,
  refusal 6/6, citation precision 13/15 and faithfulness 12/15. The run took
  710.3 s with 26 model requests, 0 retries and 0 rate limits. The hosted /ask
  answered in 27.5 s with a citation on page 8 of the Sentence-BERT paper.
